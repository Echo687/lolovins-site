Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Lolo Vins imports and distributes natural wine from small French growers. We are registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 86052268. For any question about your data, email info@lolovins.nl.

What this website collects

This website is a simple information page. It has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or tracking. All fonts and images are served from our own domain.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, automatically processes technical data such as your IP address when you visit, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. We do not use this data ourselves.

When you email us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and the content of your message only to answer you and, if you order, to handle your order. We do not share your details with others for marketing, and we delete correspondence when we no longer need it, unless the law requires us to keep it (for example for invoices).

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email info@lolovins.nl. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

When our new website and webshop go live, we will update this statement to cover orders, payments and delivery.
